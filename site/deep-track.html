Deep Track — data-driven physical simulation, actually run

Nine ideas at the heart of scientific machine learning, each rebuilt as a first-principles deep dive in plain language: a real problem, the reframe that cracks it, and a result we actually computed and ran — a network solving a differential equation with no data (error 0.9 to 0.002), SINDy reading an equation back off raw trajectories, a hybrid model cutting simulation error 122-fold, an ensemble whose error bars grow 8x exactly where it has no data. Every quantity is produced by a short program (scripts/experiments/ddps_run.py); nothing is asserted.
