High-Value Talks

Detailed first-principles writeups for seminars that best frame the field. This section starts empty and grows as we review the transcript set manually.

Talk 1 · University of Pennsylvania

Paris Perdikaris: When And Why PINNs Fail To Train

This lecture explains that physics-informed neural networks fail not because the idea of adding physics is wrong, but because the resulting optimization problem can become stiff, spectrally biased, and badly balanced across loss terms.

Talk 2 · Courant Institute / NYU

Donsub Rim: Distilling Nonlinear Shock Waves

This lecture explains why classical linear reduced-order models struggle on transport-dominated problems and how nonlinear representations can capture moving shocks and interfaces more efficiently.

Talk 4 · EPFL

Jan Hesthaven: Non-Intrusive ROMs Using Physics-Informed Neural Networks

This lecture frames neural networks as tools for removing bottlenecks in reduced-order modeling, especially when the full simulator is a black box and projection-based intrusive access is unavailable.

Talk 10 · Technical University of Munich

Nils Thuerey: Differentiable Physics Simulations For Deep Learning

This lecture argues for tightly coupling simulators and deep learning by making the simulator differentiable, so gradients from physical dynamics can guide learning, inverse problems, and control.

Talk 11 · Max Planck Institute for Dynamics of Complex Technical Systems

Pawan Goyal: Physics-Informed Learning For Nonlinear Dynamical Systems

This lecture presents operator inference as a way to learn reduced dynamical models directly from data while preserving known physical structure.

Talk 3 · Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory

Youngsoo Choi: Efficient Nonlinear Manifold Reduced Order Model

This short lecture explains how nonlinear manifold ROMs can recover accuracy on advection-dominated problems while hyper-reduction makes the nonlinear decoder practical.

Talk 5 · Rice University

Jesse Chan: Entropy Stable Schemes For Nonlinear Conservation Laws

This lecture explains entropy stability as a principled way to keep high-order discretizations of nonlinear conservation laws robust near shocks, turbulence, and under-resolved features.

Talk 6 · Sandia National Laboratories

Irina Tezaur: Schwarz Alternating Method For Concurrent Multiscale Coupling In Solid Mechanics

This lecture presents Schwarz alternating domain decomposition as a way to couple different solid-mechanics models across scales without forcing the whole domain to use one resolution or solver.

Talk 7 · University of Washington

Steven Brunton: Interpretable And Generalizable Machine Learning For Fluid Mechanics

This lecture argues that fluid ML should prefer parsimonious, interpretable, physically meaningful models that generalize beyond the data distribution.

Talk 8 · EPFL / Politecnico di Milano

Alfio Quarteroni: The Mathematical Heart

This lecture treats the heart as a coupled multiphysics, multiscale simulation problem spanning electrophysiology, mechanics, blood flow, valves, circulation, and coronary perfusion.

Talk 9 · ETH Zurich / Disney Research Studios

Byungsoo Kim: Data-Driven Methods For Fluid Simulations In Computer Graphics

This lecture explains how graphics-oriented fluid simulation uses data-driven methods to trade some physical exactness for speed, visual quality, controllability, and artist direction.

Talk 12 · University of Michigan

Karthik Duraisamy: Towards Robust, Accurate And Tractable Reduced-Order Models

This lecture frames practical ROM development around three simultaneous requirements: robustness, accuracy, and tractability for multiscale multiphysics systems.

Talk 13 · University of Muenster

Mario Ohlberger: Model Reduction With Adaptive Enrichment For Large Scale PDE-Constrained Optimization

This lecture explains adaptive enrichment as a way to use reduced models inside large-scale PDE-constrained optimization without paying the full offline cost upfront.

Talk 14 · Inria / model reduction research community

Tommaso Taddei: Registration-Based Model Reduction Of Parameterized Advection-Dominated PDEs

This lecture presents registration as a way to align moving features in advection-dominated PDEs so that a linear reduced model becomes effective in transformed coordinates.

Talk 15 · ENSAM Institute of Technology / ESI Group

Francisco Chinesta: Empowering Hybrid Twins From Physics-Informed Artificial Intelligence

This lecture describes hybrid twins as the combination of physics-based models with learned gap models that correct what the physics model misses.

Talk 16 · Carnegie Mellon University

This lecture explains implicit layers as a way to put power-system constraints, optimization problems, and control structure inside neural networks.

Talk 17 · Virginia Tech

Talk 18 · Brown University

This lecture connects PINNs and DeepONets by showing how neural networks can approximate not only functions, but functionals and operators that map functions to functions.

Talk 19 · DeepMind

Talk 20 · Argonne National Laboratory

Talk 21 · George Mason University
