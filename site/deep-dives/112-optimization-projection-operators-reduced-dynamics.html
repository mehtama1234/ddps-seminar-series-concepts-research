Talk 112 · University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

Alberto Padovan: Model Reduction via Optimization of Projection Operators and Reduced-Order Dynamics

This lecture introduces a non-intrusive framework that learns oblique projection operators and reduced-order dynamics directly from data.

YouTube source · Talk page

Core Problem

Black-box solvers make non-intrusive ROMs attractive, but standard data-driven reduction often uses orthogonal POD projections. For systems with large-amplitude transient growth, the relevant low-dimensional subspace may be easy to identify, yet orthogonal projection onto it can produce inaccurate reduced dynamics.

First-Principles Model

Projection-based ROMs need two ingredients: a low-dimensional subspace and a rule for projecting the full state onto that subspace. Orthogonal projection is simple but not always dynamically appropriate. Oblique projection can better preserve input-output or transient-growth behavior, but it is usually computed intrusively from governing equations. The proposed method learns both oblique projection operators and polynomial reduced dynamics from trajectories by solving an optimization problem on product manifolds such as Grassmann and Stiefel manifolds.

Key Claims

  • Non-intrusive model reduction is valuable for systems simulated by black-box solvers.
  • Large-amplitude transient growth can make orthogonal projections poor even when POD finds a good subspace.
  • Oblique projection operators can improve predictive accuracy.
  • Traditional oblique projections often require intrusive access to governing equations.
  • The proposed framework identifies projection operators and reduced dynamics directly from data.
  • Polynomial reduced dynamics and manifold optimization provide the mathematical structure of the fitting problem.

Important Terms

Oblique Projection

A projection onto one subspace along directions not necessarily orthogonal to that subspace.

Proper Orthogonal Decomposition

A linear reduction method that identifies high-energy modes from snapshot data.

Transient Growth

Temporary amplification of perturbations even when asymptotic dynamics may be stable.

Grassmann Manifold

The mathematical space of fixed-dimensional subspaces.

Polynomial Reduced Dynamics

Reduced equations whose right-hand side is represented by polynomial functions of reduced variables.

Connections Across The Series

  • This lecture connects non-intrusive ROMs, projection geometry, transient dynamics, and manifold optimization.
  • It complements balanced truncation and control-oriented ROM talks by emphasizing that the projection itself is part of the model.
  • It also links to black-box simulator settings where equation access is unavailable.

Practical Caution

Learning oblique projections from data can improve accuracy, but it adds identifiability and optimization challenges. The learned projection should be tested outside the training trajectories.

Takeaway

The first-principles lesson is that model reduction is not only about choosing a subspace; the projection geometry can determine whether the reduced dynamics are predictive.
