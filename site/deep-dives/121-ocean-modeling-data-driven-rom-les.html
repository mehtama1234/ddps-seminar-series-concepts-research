Talk 121 · University of Houston

Annalisa Quaini: Reducing the Cost of Ocean Modeling with a Data-Driven ROM and LES

This lecture combines POD, LSTM reduced models, and LES filtering to reduce the cost of two-layer quasi-geostrophic ocean simulation.

YouTube source · Talk page

Core Problem

Ocean models such as the two-layer quasi-geostrophic equations require large domains and high resolution to capture turbulent scales. Full simulations are expensive, but cheap models must still preserve the important potential-vorticity and stream-function dynamics.

First-Principles Model

The framework has two pieces. First, large eddy simulation with nonlinear spatial filters produces affordable training data that still represent unresolved turbulence effects. Second, a predictive parametric data-driven ROM applies POD to extract dominant modes for each field variable and trains LSTM recurrent networks to evolve the reduced coefficients. Together, LES reduces data-generation cost while the ROM reduces online prediction cost.

Key Claims

  • Two-layer quasi-geostrophic equations model stratified wind-driven ocean dynamics.
  • High-resolution ocean simulation is expensive because of domain size and turbulent scale range.
  • The proposed framework combines a predictive parametric data-driven ROM with LES-generated data.
  • Nonlinear spatial filters are used in the LES component.
  • POD extracts dominant modes for each field variable.
  • LSTM recurrent neural networks model the temporal evolution of reduced coordinates.

Important Terms

Two-Layer Quasi-Geostrophic Equations

A simplified ocean model describing stratified rotating flow using potential vorticity and stream function.

Large Eddy Simulation

A turbulence simulation approach that resolves large scales and models smaller scales.

POD

Proper Orthogonal Decomposition, a method for extracting dominant modes from data.

LSTM

Long Short-Term Memory, a recurrent neural network architecture for sequence modeling.

Potential Vorticity

A conserved or nearly conserved quantity central to rotating stratified fluid dynamics.

Connections Across The Series

  • This talk connects ROMs, turbulent geophysical flows, LES, and recurrent neural networks.
  • It complements climate and ocean surrogate modeling discussions by pairing physics-based filtering with learned temporal evolution.
  • It also links to multiscale modeling because LES and ROMs address different cost bottlenecks.

Practical Caution

The ROM depends on the quality of LES-generated training data. If the filter misses important subgrid effects, the LSTM may learn a biased reduced dynamics.

Takeaway

The first-principles lesson is that ocean-model cost can be reduced by using physics-aware coarse simulation to feed learned reduced dynamics.
