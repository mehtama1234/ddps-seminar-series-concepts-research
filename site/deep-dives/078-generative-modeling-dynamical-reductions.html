Talk 78 · University of California, Santa Barbara

Paul Atzberger: Generative Machine Learning For Data-Driven Modeling And Reductions

This lecture surveys generative models for nonlinear dynamical-system reduction, including geometric VAEs for physically meaningful latent representations and stochastic dynamic GANs for probabilistic SDE models.

YouTube source · Talk page

Core Problem

Soft materials, complex fluids, and biophysical simulations involve many spatial and temporal scales plus stochastic fluctuations. Reduced models need to represent nonlinear dynamics and uncertainty, but generic latent-variable models may ignore topology, smoothness, physical principles, and stochastic numerical stability.

First-Principles Model

A generative reduction learns a simulator-like representation from observations or high-fidelity simulations. Geometric VAEs impose inductive biases so the latent representation respects topology, smoothness, and physics. SDYN-GANs learn probabilistic dynamics in SDE form, including drift, diffusion, and stable multi-step stochastic integrators. The representation is not just compressed data; it is a learned dynamical model.

Key Claims

  • Generative ML creates opportunities for reduced-order and multiscale modeling of nonlinear scientific simulations.
  • Physical principles and prior scientific knowledge should be incorporated as inductive biases.
  • Geometric variational autoencoders can learn representations with topological information, smoothness, and physical adherence.
  • GD-VAEs can support reductions of high-dimensional dynamical systems and nonlinear PDEs.
  • SDYN-GANs learn probabilistic dynamical models from observations of stochastic systems.
  • SDYN-GAN representations use SDEs and stable multi-step stochastic numerical integrators for simulation.

Important Terms

Generative Model

A model that learns to generate samples or trajectories resembling the data distribution.

Geometric Variational Autoencoder

A VAE designed to preserve geometric, topological, smoothness, or physical structure in the latent representation.

Stochastic Dynamic GAN

A generative adversarial model for learning stochastic dynamical representations.

Stochastic Differential Equation

A differential equation with random terms that models stochastic dynamics.

Multi-Step Stochastic Integrator

A numerical method that advances stochastic dynamics stably across multiple time steps.

Connections Across The Series

  • This lecture connects generative modeling with ROMs, stochastic model reduction, and manifold learning.
  • It complements information-geometry and charting-dynamics talks by emphasizing structure in learned latent spaces.
  • It also links to UQ because stochastic dynamics require probabilistic reduced models, not just deterministic surrogates.

Practical Caution

A generative model can produce plausible trajectories while violating physical laws or stochastic stability. Scientific reductions need structural constraints, not just visual or distributional realism.

Takeaway

The first-principles lesson is that generative SciML should learn structured dynamics, not just samples. The latent space must support simulation.
