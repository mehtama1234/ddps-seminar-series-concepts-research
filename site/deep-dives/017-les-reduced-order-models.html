Talk 17 · Virginia Tech

Traian Iliescu: Large Eddy Simulation Reduced Order Models

This lecture explains LES-ROMs as reduced-order models for turbulent flows that explicitly address unresolved scales through ROM filtering and closure modeling.

YouTube source · Talk page

Core Problem

Turbulent flows contain many interacting scales. A low-dimensional ROM can represent only the largest structures, so under-resolved modes can feed back onto the resolved dynamics and destabilize or bias the prediction. Standard projection ROMs do not automatically solve this closure problem.

First-Principles Model

Large eddy simulation separates resolved large scales from unresolved small scales using a filter, then models the effect of the unresolved scales through a closure term. LES-ROM applies the same idea to reduced-order models: define ROM spatial filters, evolve the resolved ROM modes, and add closure models for the effects of truncated modes.

Key Claims

  • Reduced-order modeling for turbulence is not a settled field.
  • Galerkin ROMs expand the solution in data-driven basis functions and project the PDE onto that basis.
  • When nonlinear equations are filtered, unresolved scales create a closure term that must be modeled.
  • ROM closures can be functional or structural, echoing LES closure terminology.
  • Pressure stabilization and other practical fixes may be less elegant than theory but useful in real computations.
  • Scaling laws for ROM closures may be problem-specific rather than universal across all Reynolds numbers and flows.

Important Terms

Large Eddy Simulation

A turbulence simulation strategy that resolves large scales and models the effect of unresolved small scales.

ROM Filter

A reduced-order analogue of a spatial filter that defines which scales the ROM can resolve.

Closure Problem

The need to model the effect of unresolved or truncated scales on the resolved variables.

Eddy Viscosity

A functional closure idea that adds dissipative effects representing unresolved turbulent mixing.

Data-Driven Variational Multiscale ROM

A ROM closure approach that combines variational multiscale ideas with data-driven modeling.

Connections Across The Series

  • This connects entropy-stable fluid schemes, interpretable fluid ML, and ROMs around the common issue of unresolved scales.
  • It is a central caution for operator learning and neural surrogates of turbulence: the missing small scales still act on the large scales.
  • It also gives a first-principles reason why long-rollout validation matters for turbulent surrogate models.

Practical Caution

Do not expect one ROM closure coefficient or scaling law to work across arbitrary Reynolds numbers, geometries, and flow regimes. Turbulent ROM validation must be tied to the target flow family.

Takeaway

The first-principles lesson is that turbulence ROMs are filtered models. If the unresolved scales are ignored, the reduced model is not merely lower-dimensional; it is missing physics.
