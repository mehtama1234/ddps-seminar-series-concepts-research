Talk 89 · Yale University

Lu Lu: Deep Neural Operators With Reliable Extrapolation

This lecture presents DeepONet and related neural-operator extensions for multiphysics and multiscale problems, with special attention to quantifying and improving extrapolation beyond interpolation regimes.

YouTube source · Talk page

Core Problem

Neural operators can learn solution maps for complex systems, but deep learning models are usually strongest in interpolation. Scientific applications need extrapolation across regimes, fidelities, inputs, and coupled physics, where naive operator models may fail silently.

First-Principles Model

DeepONet learns nonlinear operators by combining a branch network that encodes input functions and a trunk network that encodes evaluation locations. Extensions adapt this basic operator-learning template to multiphysics, POD-reduced representations, multiple-input operators, Fourier structure, and multifidelity data. Reliable extrapolation requires understanding the complexity of moving outside the training regime and building workflows that detect or reduce that gap.

Key Claims

  • Neural networks can approximate nonlinear operators, not only finite-dimensional functions.
  • DeepONet learns operators representing deterministic and stochastic differential equations.
  • DeepM&Mnet extends the operator-learning idea to multiphysics problems.
  • POD-DeepONet combines proper orthogonal decomposition with operator learning.
  • MIONet targets multiple-input operators, while multifidelity DeepONet combines data of different cost and accuracy.
  • Reliable scientific use requires quantifying extrapolation complexity and building workflows for extrapolation.

Important Terms

DeepONet

A deep operator network architecture for learning mappings from functions to functions.

POD-DeepONet

A DeepONet variant that uses proper orthogonal decomposition to reduce output representation.

MIONet

A neural operator architecture for multiple-input operators.

Multifidelity Learning

Learning from data sources with different costs and accuracies.

Extrapolation Complexity

A measure or characterization of how difficult it is for a model to predict outside its training domain.

Connections Across The Series

  • This talk expands the neural-operator thread beyond FNO-style discussions to DeepONet families.
  • It connects to Anima Anandkumar's operator talk through function-space learning, but emphasizes extrapolation reliability.
  • It also links to multifidelity, multiscale, and multiphysics themes across the series.

Practical Caution

Operator learning does not automatically solve extrapolation. A model can be excellent across sampled functions and unreliable under new physics, scales, or coupled regimes.

Takeaway

The first-principles lesson is that neural operators must be judged by the domain of operators they can reliably generalize across, not just by interpolation error.
