Talk 5 · Rice University

Jesse Chan: Entropy Stable Schemes For Nonlinear Conservation Laws

This lecture explains entropy stability as a principled way to keep high-order discretizations of nonlinear conservation laws robust near shocks, turbulence, and under-resolved features.

YouTube source · Talk page

Core Problem

High-order numerical methods can be accurate on smooth PDE solutions but fragile on nonlinear conservation laws. Shocks, turbulence, and under-resolved gradients can cause blow-up unless the method adds filtering, limiting, or artificial viscosity. Those fixes can work, but they create a tuning tradeoff between robustness and accuracy.

First-Principles Model

A nonlinear conservation law transports conserved variables through fluxes. For many physical systems there is an entropy or energy-like quantity that should satisfy an inequality. An entropy-stable discretization is built so the semi-discrete numerical scheme respects the analogous entropy inequality, making stability a structural property rather than an after-the-fact patch.

Key Claims

  • Burgers, shallow water, Euler, Navier-Stokes, and MHD systems can be viewed through nonlinear conservation laws with entropy structure.
  • High-order DG methods are attractive because they combine geometric flexibility with high-order accuracy.
  • Under-resolved shocks and turbulence make naive high-order schemes non-robust.
  • Traditional stabilization through artificial viscosity, filtering, or slope limiting can reduce accuracy and require tuning.
  • Entropy-stable schemes use entropy variables, numerical fluxes, and discrete entropy inequalities to build robustness into the scheme.
  • The framework can be extended beyond collocation-style methods toward modal DG and reduced-order modeling.

Important Terms

Nonlinear Conservation Law

A PDE expressing that conserved quantities change through fluxes across boundaries.

Entropy Inequality

A mathematical inequality for an energy-like convex entropy quantity that physically relevant solutions should satisfy.

Entropy Variables

Variables obtained by differentiating the entropy with respect to conservative variables.

Discontinuous Galerkin Method

A high-order finite-element-style discretization that represents solutions locally and couples cells through numerical fluxes.

Artificial Viscosity

Added numerical diffusion used to stabilize under-resolved features, often at the cost of accuracy.

Connections Across The Series

  • This lecture adds the numerical-analysis side of robustness to the ML-heavy talks: physical validity depends on discretization structure too.
  • It connects to fluid simulation and reduced-order modeling because learned or reduced models inherit stability issues from the equations they approximate.
  • It is a useful counterweight to black-box surrogates: before learning the solution, understand what the numerical scheme must preserve.

Practical Caution

Do not evaluate a learned fluid surrogate only by short-horizon error. If the underlying conservation-law structure or entropy behavior is wrong, under-resolved shocks and turbulence can create unstable or nonphysical rollouts.

Takeaway

Entropy stability is the idea that robustness should be built from the physics of the PDE, not bolted on after the scheme starts failing.
