Talk 134 · University of Washington

Jingwei Hu: Structure-Preserving Particle Method for Collisional Plasmas

This lecture introduces a particle method for collisional plasmas that preserves conservation laws and entropy dissipation.

YouTube source · Talk page

Core Problem

The Vlasov-Maxwell-Landau equation models plasma transport, electromagnetic fields, and Coulomb collisions, but numerical particle methods must preserve physical invariants. A method that violates mass, momentum, energy, or entropy behavior can give physically wrong plasma dynamics.

First-Principles Model

The method starts from a variational formulation of the Landau collision operator and regularizes it to obtain a particle discretization. Collisional effects appear as a deterministic effective force that can be coupled with classical PIC. The discretization conserves mass, momentum, and energy while dissipating entropy, matching the physics of collisional relaxation.

Key Claims

  • The Vlasov-Maxwell-Landau equation is a first-principles plasma model.
  • The new particle method models transport, electromagnetic fields, and Coulomb collisions together.
  • The method arises from regularizing the variational formulation of the Landau collision operator.
  • The discretization conserves mass, momentum, and energy.
  • It dissipates entropy, preserving the thermodynamic direction of collisions.
  • The deterministic effective collision force can couple naturally with PIC.

Important Terms

Vlasov-Maxwell-Landau Equation

A kinetic plasma model coupling particle distributions, electromagnetic fields, and collisional effects.

Landau Collision Operator

An operator modeling Coulomb collisions in plasma kinetic theory.

Structure-Preserving Method

A numerical method designed to preserve invariants or physical laws of the continuous system.

Entropy Dissipation

The monotonic decay of entropy-related quantities consistent with irreversible collisional relaxation.

Particle-in-Cell

A plasma simulation method coupling particles with grid-based fields.

Connections Across The Series

  • This talk connects structure-preserving numerics, kinetic theory, and plasma particle simulation.
  • It complements moment-enhanced PIC by focusing on collisional invariants and entropy.
  • It also reinforces a broad DDPS theme: numerical schemes should preserve the physics that define the model.

Practical Caution

Preserving invariants is necessary but not sufficient. The method must also be tested for accuracy and efficiency on regimes such as damping, instability, and shock-like plasma behavior.

Takeaway

The first-principles lesson is that plasma particle methods should preserve the conservation and dissipation laws of the kinetic equation they approximate.
