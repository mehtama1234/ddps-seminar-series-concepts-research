Talk 102 · Purdue University

Guang Lin: Towards Third Wave AI: Interpretable, Robust Trustworthy Machine Learning

This lecture frames trustworthy scientific AI as a combination of neural operators, uncertainty quantification, physics knowledge, and causal modeling.

YouTube source · Talk page

Core Problem

Scientific and public-health decisions need real-time prediction, surveillance, and defense, but opaque models without uncertainty or interpretability are risky. The question is how to make machine learning robust enough for high-consequence dynamical systems.

First-Principles Model

Third Wave AI is presented as a move beyond pure data fitting toward models that combine learning, uncertainty, physics, and causal structure. Neural operator architectures learn mappings between functions while producing uncertainty estimates. Multifidelity, federated, and Bayesian variants let models use data from multiple sources, preserve distributed data constraints, and quantify uncertainty. Physics knowledge and causal discovery help make the resulting models more interpretable and trustworthy.

Key Claims

  • Trustworthy AI requires prediction plus uncertainty, robustness, and interpretability.
  • Neural operator networks can learn nonlinear operators from functions.
  • Bayesian neural operators provide simultaneous uncertainty estimates.
  • Multifidelity and federated architectures address heterogeneous data sources and distributed data constraints.
  • Physics knowledge can be incorporated to design more interpretable models.
  • Applications include COVID-19 prediction with uncertainty and causal discovery for Alzheimer's disease.

Important Terms

Third Wave AI

An AI framing that emphasizes contextual, interpretable, robust, and trustworthy systems rather than black-box pattern recognition alone.

Neural Operator

A neural architecture designed to learn mappings between functions or fields.

Bayesian Neural Operator

A neural operator with Bayesian uncertainty modeling.

Multifidelity Learning

Learning that combines data of different cost, resolution, or accuracy.

Causal Discovery

Methods that infer possible causal relationships from data rather than only correlations.

Connections Across The Series

  • This talk connects neural operators, UQ, federated learning, and trustworthy scientific machine learning.
  • It complements earlier safety and reliability discussions by treating uncertainty and interpretability as core model outputs.
  • It also links scientific ML to public-health and biomedical decision support.

Practical Caution

Trustworthy AI is not achieved by adding uncertainty bars alone. The uncertainty model, causal assumptions, data provenance, and deployment feedback loop all need validation.

Takeaway

The first-principles lesson is that scientific AI becomes deployable only when prediction is paired with uncertainty, interpretability, and domain structure.
