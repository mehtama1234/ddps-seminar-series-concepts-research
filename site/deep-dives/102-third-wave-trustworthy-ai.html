Talk 102 · Purdue University

Guang Lin: Towards Third Wave AI: Interpretable, Robust Trustworthy Machine Learning

This lecture frames trustworthy scientific AI as a combination of neural operators (models that learn mappings between functions), uncertainty quantification (estimates of how sure the model should be), physics knowledge, and causal modeling (finding what causes what in data).

YouTube source · Talk page

Core Problem

Scientific and public-health decisions need real-time prediction, surveillance, and defense, but models that hide their inner workings without uncertainty or interpretability (how you can understand what the model is doing) are risky. The question is how to make machine learning reliable enough for high-stakes dynamical systems where errors have real consequences.

First-Principles Model

Third Wave AI moves beyond pure data fitting toward models that combine learning, uncertainty, physics, and causal structure. Neural operator architectures learn mappings between functions while also producing uncertainty estimates. Multifidelity approaches combine data from multiple sources with different costs or accuracy levels, federated learning preserves distributed data privacy constraints, and Bayesian methods quantify how certain the model should be. Physics knowledge and causal discovery (methods that infer what causes what from data) help make the resulting models more interpretable and trustworthy.

Key Claims

  • Trustworthy AI requires prediction plus uncertainty, reliability, and the ability to understand how the model works.
  • Neural operator networks can learn nonlinear operators from functions.
  • Bayesian neural operators provide simultaneous uncertainty estimates.
  • Multifidelity and federated architectures handle data from different sources and respect privacy constraints on distributed data.
  • Physics knowledge can be incorporated to design models you can understand better.
  • Applications include COVID-19 prediction with uncertainty and causal discovery for Alzheimer's disease.

Important Terms

Third Wave AI

An AI approach that emphasizes systems that are contextual, interpretable (you can understand what they're doing), reliable, and trustworthy rather than models that work but hide their logic.

Neural Operator

A neural architecture designed to learn mappings between functions or fields.

Bayesian Neural Operator

A neural operator that adds uncertainty modeling, so it can say how confident it is.

Multifidelity Learning

Learning that combines data of different cost, resolution, or accuracy.

Causal Discovery

Methods that infer what actually causes what from data rather than only measuring correlations (whether things move together).

Connections Across The Series

  • This talk connects neural operators, uncertainty quantification, federated learning, and trustworthy scientific machine learning.
  • It complements earlier safety and reliability discussions by treating uncertainty and interpretability as core model outputs.
  • It also links scientific ML to public-health and biomedical decision support.

Practical Caution

Trustworthy AI is not achieved by adding uncertainty bars alone. The uncertainty model, causal assumptions, data quality, and feedback from real deployments all need validation.

Takeaway

The first-principles lesson is that scientific AI becomes ready to deploy only when prediction is paired with uncertainty, interpretability (showing your reasoning), and domain structure.
