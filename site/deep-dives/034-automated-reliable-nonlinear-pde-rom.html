Talk 34 · University of Toronto

Masayuki Yano: Reliable, Efficient, And Automated Model Reduction Of Parametrized Nonlinear PDEs

This lecture presents automated goal-oriented ROM construction for parametrized nonlinear PDEs by jointly controlling discretization, reduced-basis, and hyper-reduction errors.

YouTube source · Talk page

Core Problem

Engineering studies need fast PDE predictions for many configurations, but reduced models can be unreliable if the full-order discretization, reduced basis, or reduced quadrature is undertrained. Manual ROM construction also becomes expensive and fragile for nonlinear aerodynamic problems.

First-Principles Model

A goal-oriented ROM targets a quantity of interest, such as lift or drag. Adaptive DG controls spatial discretization error; reduced-basis spaces compress the parametric solution manifold; empirical quadrature hyper-reduces residual evaluation; and dual-weighted residual estimates guide training. A greedy algorithm adds training points until error estimates meet a user tolerance.

Key Claims

  • Reliable ROM construction must account for finite-element, reduced-basis, and reduced-quadrature errors.
  • Adaptive high-order DG methods can make offline snapshot generation more efficient.
  • Dual-weighted residual methods estimate errors in quantities of interest.
  • Empirical quadrature provides hyper-reduction by evaluating residuals on a subset of elements.
  • Greedy training can automatically build finite-element spaces, reduced bases, and reduced quadrature rules.
  • High-dimensional parameters and shock-dominated flows remain challenging.

Important Terms

Goal-Oriented Model Reduction

ROM construction focused on controlling error in specific quantities of interest.

Adaptive DG

Discontinuous Galerkin discretization with mesh or polynomial refinement guided by error estimates.

Dual-Weighted Residual

An error estimation method that weights residuals by adjoint sensitivity to a quantity of interest.

Empirical Quadrature

A hyper-reduction method that approximates integrals or residuals using selected elements and weights.

Greedy Training

An iterative procedure that adds the most informative parameter samples or basis elements according to an error criterion.

Connections Across The Series

  • This is the automated reliability counterpart to earlier ROM talks: it asks how to generate the ROM without manual tuning.
  • It connects to adaptive enrichment and trust-region ROMs through error-controlled training.
  • It also connects to aerodynamics and shock discussions because convection-dominated PDEs stress both discretization and reduction.

Practical Caution

The talk uses certified language carefully: some quantities are estimates rather than rigorous bounds. Automated workflows still need independent validation, especially for high-dimensional or shock-dominated cases.

Takeaway

The first-principles lesson is that automation requires an error model. A ROM builder must know what it is reducing, what error it is controlling, and where to sample next.
