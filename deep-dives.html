High-Value Talks

Detailed first-principles writeups for seminars that best frame the field. This section starts empty and grows as we review the transcript set manually.

Talk 1 · University of Pennsylvania

Paris Perdikaris: When And Why PINNs Fail To Train

This lecture explains that physics-informed neural networks fail not because the idea of adding physics is wrong, but because the resulting optimization problem can become stiff, spectrally biased, and badly balanced across loss terms.

Talk 2 · Courant Institute / NYU

Donsub Rim: Distilling Nonlinear Shock Waves

This lecture explains why classical linear reduced-order models struggle on transport-dominated problems and how nonlinear representations can capture moving shocks and interfaces more efficiently.

Talk 4 · EPFL

Jan Hesthaven: Non-Intrusive ROMs Using Physics-Informed Neural Networks

This lecture frames neural networks as tools for removing bottlenecks in reduced-order modeling, especially when the full simulator is a black box and projection-based intrusive access is unavailable.

Talk 10 · Technical University of Munich

Nils Thuerey: Differentiable Physics Simulations For Deep Learning

This lecture argues for tightly coupling simulators and deep learning by making the simulator differentiable, so gradients from physical dynamics can guide learning, inverse problems, and control.

Talk 11 · Max Planck Institute for Dynamics of Complex Technical Systems

Pawan Goyal: Physics-Informed Learning For Nonlinear Dynamical Systems

This lecture presents operator inference as a way to learn reduced dynamical models directly from data while preserving known physical structure.
