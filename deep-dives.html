Detailed first-principles writeups for seminars that best frame the field. This section starts empty and grows as we review the transcript set manually.

Talk 1 · University of Pennsylvania

Paris Perdikaris: When And Why PINNs Fail To Train

This lecture explains that physics-informed neural networks fail not because adding physics to training is wrong, but because the resulting math problem for the optimizer can become stiff, spectrally biased, and badly balanced—where one loss term overpowers others.

Talk 2 · Courant Institute / NYU

Donsub Rim: Distilling Nonlinear Shock Waves

This lecture explains why classical linear reduced-order models fail on transport-dominated problems and how nonlinear representations can capture moving shocks and interfaces more efficiently.

Talk 4 · EPFL

Jan Hesthaven: Non-Intrusive ROMs Using Physics-Informed Neural Networks

This lecture shows how neural networks can replace mathematical projections in reduced-order modeling when you cannot look inside the full simulator and must learn from its input-output data alone.

Talk 10 · Technical University of Munich

Nils Thuerey: Differentiable Physics Simulations For Deep Learning

This lecture shows how to connect simulators and deep learning by making the simulator compute gradients, so that signals from physical laws can guide learning, answer inverse problems, and improve control.

Talk 11 · Max Planck Institute for Dynamics of Complex Technical Systems

Pawan Goyal: Physics-Informed Learning For Nonlinear Dynamical Systems

Operator inference learns reduced dynamical models from data while keeping the known physics structure built in.

Talk 3 · Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory

Youngsoo Choi: Efficient Nonlinear Manifold Reduced Order Model

This short lecture explains how nonlinear manifold ROMs (a way to turn a huge simulation into a small one using a curved space instead of a straight one) can stay accurate on flows where material moves fast, and how hyper-reduction makes the curved-space decoder cheap enough to use.

Talk 5 · Rice University

Jesse Chan: Entropy Stable Schemes For Nonlinear Conservation Laws

This lecture explains entropy stability as a way to build high-order discretizations of nonlinear conservation laws that stay reliable near shocks, turbulence, and features too small to resolve.

Talk 6 · Sandia National Laboratories

Irina Tezaur: Schwarz Alternating Method For Concurrent Multiscale Coupling In Solid Mechanics

This lecture presents Schwarz alternating domain decomposition as a way to couple different solid-mechanics models across scales without forcing the whole domain to use one resolution or solver.

Talk 7 · University of Washington

Steven Brunton: Interpretable And Generalizable Machine Learning For Fluid Mechanics

This lecture argues that fluid ML should prefer simple, interpretable, physically meaningful models that work well on new problems not seen during training.

Talk 8 · EPFL / Politecnico di Milano

Alfio Quarteroni: The Mathematical Heart

This lecture treats the heart as a coupled multiphysics, multiscale simulation problem spanning electrophysiology, mechanics, blood flow, valves, circulation, and coronary perfusion.

Talk 9 · ETH Zurich / Disney Research Studios

Byungsoo Kim: Data-Driven Methods For Fluid Simulations In Computer Graphics

This lecture explains how computer graphics simulates fluids using data-driven methods to speed up calculation while keeping the look convincing, giving artists control, and letting animators direct the result.

Talk 12 · University of Michigan

Karthik Duraisamy: Towards Robust, Accurate And Tractable Reduced-Order Models

This lecture organizes practical reduced-order modeling around three competing needs: building models that stay accurate even when conditions change, match the real physics with low error, and run fast enough to be useful for design and prediction.

Talk 13 · University of Muenster

Mario Ohlberger: Model Reduction With Adaptive Enrichment For Large Scale PDE-Constrained Optimization

This lecture explains adaptive enrichment as a way to use reduced models in large-scale PDE-constrained optimization without paying the full offline cost upfront.

Talk 14 · Inria / model reduction research community

Tommaso Taddei: Registration-Based Model Reduction Of Parameterized Advection-Dominated PDEs

This lecture presents registration—a technique to align moving features in advection-dominated PDEs (partial differential equations where material flows and carries features)—so that a linear reduced model works well in the aligned coordinates.

Talk 15 · ENSAM Institute of Technology / ESI Group

Francisco Chinesta: Empowering Hybrid Twins From Physics-Informed Artificial Intelligence

A hybrid twin combines physics-based models with learned models that predict what the physics model misses, correcting the gap between simulation and reality.

Talk 16 · Carnegie Mellon University

Talk 17 · Virginia Tech
