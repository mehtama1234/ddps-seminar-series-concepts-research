Talk 21 · George Mason University

Harbir Antil: Applications Of Fractional Operators From Optimal Control To Machine Learning

This lecture introduces fractional operators as nonlocal models useful in imaging, geophysics, optimal control, data science, and deep neural networks.

YouTube source · Talk page

Core Problem

Many physical and data systems have nonlocal behavior, long-range correlations, memory, or anomalous diffusion that classical integer-order differential operators do not express naturally. Modeling those effects with local derivatives alone can miss the interaction structure of heterogeneous materials, images, or reacting flows.

First-Principles Model

A fractional operator generalizes differentiation or diffusion to non-integer order. Instead of only local derivatives, the operator can encode interactions across distance or history. The fractional Laplacian is one example: it can smooth or diffuse information in a way that reflects nonlocal structure. Fractional exponents can also become parameters to infer or random variables for uncertainty.

Key Claims

  • Fractional calculus is useful for anomalous diffusion and systems with long-range or hereditary effects.
  • Fractional operators can replace nonlinear image-regularization models with tractable linear fractional PDEs in some settings.
  • Optimal control of fractional PDEs introduces new analysis and computational challenges.
  • Unknown fractional exponents can be modeled as parameters or random variables.
  • Fractional operators can be used inside deep neural network architectures.
  • Chemical reacting flows provide a difficult time-scale example where fractional neural ideas may help.

Important Terms

Fractional Operator

A differential or integral operator of non-integer order, often encoding nonlocal behavior.

Fractional Laplacian

A nonlocal generalization of the Laplace operator used in diffusion, imaging, and PDE models.

Anomalous Diffusion

Diffusion behavior that does not follow the standard local Brownian diffusion model.

Hereditary Property

A dependence on past states or history, often modeled with fractional dynamics.

Fractional DNN

A deep neural network architecture or dynamics inspired by fractional-order operators.

Connections Across The Series

  • This talk broadens the operator-learning discussion by emphasizing operators with nonlocal physics.
  • It connects to optimal control and UQ because fractional exponents and nonlocal dynamics can become uncertain control-relevant parameters.
  • It also complements graph networks: both are ways to represent interactions beyond a fixed local grid stencil.

Practical Caution

Fractional models are powerful but introduce modeling choices: the fractional order, boundary treatment, and numerical realization all affect the behavior. They should be justified by the physical nonlocality, not added as a generic complexity knob.

Takeaway

The first-principles lesson is that not all dynamics are local. Fractional operators give scientific ML a language for nonlocality, memory, and anomalous transport.
