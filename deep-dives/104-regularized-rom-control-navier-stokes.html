Talk 104 · Universita Cattolica del Sacro Cuore

Francesco Ballarin: Regularized Reduced Order Models for Control of Navier-Stokes Equations

This lecture studies how regularization and stabilization make reduced-order models more reliable for controlling convection-dominated Navier-Stokes flows.

YouTube source · Talk page

Core Problem

Convection-dominated incompressible Navier-Stokes simulations can produce spurious oscillations when under-resolved, and ROMs inherit an even harder version of this problem because the reduced basis may not represent all stabilizing scales. Control adds another difficulty: the ROM must not only simulate the flow but guide it toward a target configuration.

First-Principles Model

A full-order stabilized discretization uses numerical regularization to control unresolved scales. A ROM compresses the flow field, but the compression can remove dissipation and destabilize the reduced dynamics. The talk compares two regularized ROM strategies: evolve-filter-relax, where a reduced state is evolved, filtered, and relaxed to suppress spurious content, and approximate deconvolution, where filtering is paired with a reconstruction of unresolved information. These ideas are then tied to feedback control for Navier-Stokes systems.

Key Claims

  • Stabilization is standard in under-resolved full-order flow simulation, but its ROM counterpart is less well understood.
  • Convection-dominated and marginally resolved flows are especially sensitive to missing scales.
  • Control problems require the ROM to remain accurate under feedback, not only under open-loop simulation.
  • Evolve-filter-relax regularization can be used inside a reduced-order feedback control setting.
  • Approximate-deconvolution regularization offers another way to compensate for filtering effects.
  • The right ROM must balance speed, stability, and control-relevant accuracy.

Important Terms

Navier-Stokes Equations

The PDEs governing incompressible or compressible fluid flow.

Convection-Dominated Flow

A flow regime where transport dominates diffusion, often creating sharp features and numerical instability.

Evolve-Filter-Relax ROM

A regularized ROM that evolves a reduced state, filters it, and relaxes the result to improve stability.

Approximate Deconvolution

A technique that attempts to reconstruct unfiltered information from filtered variables.

Feedback Control

A control strategy that uses the current system state to choose actions that drive the system toward a target.

Connections Across The Series

  • This lecture connects ROMs, flow control, numerical stabilization, and closure modeling.
  • It complements operator-inference and projection-method talks by focusing on what happens when unresolved scales destabilize a reduced controller.
  • It also links to digital twins because controlled flow systems need fast surrogates with stable online behavior.

Practical Caution

A regularized ROM can be stable but biased. For control, the stabilizing term must preserve the control-relevant dynamics rather than only damping visible oscillations.

Takeaway

The first-principles lesson is that reduced flow control needs stabilization designed for the reduced dynamics, not just compression of the full solver.
