Talk 135 · ETH Zurich

Siddhartha Mishra: AI for Data-Driven Simulations in Physics

This lecture covers neural PDE surrogates and neural operators that speed up simulations used across physics and engineering.

YouTube source · Talk page

Core Problem

PDEs (partial differential equations—rules that describe how things change in space and time) are essential to physics and engineering, but solving them precisely with traditional numerical methods is very expensive. Fast AI-based replacements offer a cheaper option, but they must learn to predict solutions accurately even when the starting conditions, physical constants, or geometry change.

First-Principles Model

Neural operators learn the PDE solution operator from data—they map from inputs like initial conditions, coefficients, sources, or geometry to the resulting solution fields. The talk reviews neural operators built with convolution (sliding filters across data) and attention (learning which parts matter most), graph structures for irregular meshes, transformers for arbitrary domains, and conditional diffusion models (methods that build solutions by removing noise step by step based on input conditions).

Key Claims

  • PDEs describe how a huge range of physical and engineering systems behave.
  • Traditional numerical methods for solving PDEs are often too slow for practical use.
  • AI-based neural PDE surrogates aim to give both speed and accuracy.
  • Neural operators are a major machine learning method for PDEs.
  • Convolution and attention designs produce leading neural operators today.
  • Graph, transformer, and diffusion-based models extend PDE learning to irregular domains and generative settings where we sample new solutions.

Important Terms

Neural Operator

A model that learns mappings between function spaces—often from PDE inputs (starting conditions, coefficients) to solutions.

PDE Surrogate

A trained approximation that predicts PDE solutions much faster than running a numerical solver.

Graph Neural Operator

A neural operator that uses graph connections to handle irregular domains or irregular meshes.

Transformer Architecture

An attention-based neural design used here to learn PDE solutions and handle interactions across arbitrary geometries.

Conditional Diffusion Model

A generative model that builds outputs step by step by removing noise, guided by input conditions.

Connections Across The Series

  • This talk pulls together many DDPS threads: PDE surrogates, neural operators, attention, graphs, and data-driven physics.
  • It connects to talks on operator networks, differentiable solvers, and latent-space methods.
  • It also links to broader interests in AI scaling because neural operators are a domain-specific foundation-model direction for physics.

Practical Caution

Neural PDE surrogates must be tested to ensure they conserve key quantities, stay stable, capture uncertainty, and work on situations the model never saw during training. Speed means nothing if the model misses rare or extreme physics.

Takeaway

The core idea is that neural operators try to learn the simulator itself—the map that takes in a PDE problem's starting state and parameters and produces the solution.
