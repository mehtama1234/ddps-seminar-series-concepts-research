Talk 88 · ETH Zurich

Eleni Chatzi: Twinning And Model Discovery For Engineered Systems

This lecture frames structural health monitoring and digital twinning as hybrid model-discovery problems where data-driven sensing must be integrated with mechanics to assess, forecast, and act on engineered structures.

YouTube source · Talk page

Core Problem

Modern engineered structures are interconnected, exposed to variable loads, and operated in adverse environments. Basic SHM can detect anomalies, but higher-level tasks such as damage quantification, residual-life prognosis, and preventive action require more than black-box data fitting because the monitored structure must be represented as it is now and under future stressors.

First-Principles Model

A structural twin is a live representation of a physical asset. Sparse sensors provide measurements, while mechanics-based models propagate that information to unmeasured locations and future conditions. Model discovery identifies damage, parameters, or structure-state variables that make the model match reality. The twin becomes useful when it can detect, localize, quantify, forecast, and recommend actions.

Key Claims

  • SHM forms a hierarchy: detect damage, localize it, quantify it, and eventually prognose residual life.
  • Purely data-driven methods are lacking for higher-level SHM tasks such as damage assessment and prognosis.
  • Digital twins of monitored structures require physics-based representations grounded in mechanics.
  • Hybrid SHM can represent a structure as-is rather than only as-designed.
  • Augmented twin representations can anticipate performance under future stressors.
  • A useful twin should advise preventive and remedial actions, not only visualize measurements.

Important Terms

Structural Health Monitoring

Sensing and modeling methods for detecting, localizing, quantifying, and prognosing damage in structures.

Augmented Twin

A digital twin enhanced with data-driven updates and physics-based representations of the actual structure.

Residual Life

The remaining useful life of a system before unacceptable degradation or failure.

Full-Field Reconstruction

Estimating structural response across the full object from sparse sensor measurements.

Model Discovery

Learning model components, states, or parameters that explain observed structural behavior.

Connections Across The Series

  • This lecture connects digital twins, SHM, uncertainty, and model discovery in a deployment-oriented structural setting.
  • It complements guided-wave SHM by moving from detection/localization toward prognosis and action.
  • It also links to ROM and surrogate talks because twins need fast but mechanics-aware representations.

Practical Caution

A dashboard with sensor data is not a digital twin. Higher-level SHM needs mechanics, uncertainty, and future-load reasoning before it can support maintenance decisions.

Takeaway

The first-principles lesson is that twinning is state estimation plus mechanics plus decision support. Sensors alone do not create a useful twin.
