Talk 79 · Texas A&M University

Eduardo Gildin: Guided Deep Learning Manifold Linearization Of Porous Media Flow

This lecture presents physics-aware manifold linearization for porous-media flow, using non-intrusive reduction, autoencoders, and Koopman-style lifting for reservoir digital twin operations.

YouTube source · Talk page

Core Problem

Reservoir simulators are expensive because nonlinear porous-media equations require iterative solves at each time step. Digital twin operations need fast closed-loop simulation, optimization, and control, but fully intrusive access to simulator internals is often unavailable or impractical.

First-Principles Model

The strategy is to learn a reduced representation where the nonlinear reservoir dynamics behave more linearly. E2CO combines data-driven model reduction and DNNs to match both state trajectories and input-output behavior non-intrusively. A second route augments state space and uses an autoencoder to map reservoir equations to a reduced manifold where a Koopman operator approximates linear evolution, avoiding some nonlinear iteration cost.

Key Claims

  • Reservoir digital twin operations require fast simulation for closed-loop management, drilling, and optimization.
  • Physics-aware ML can endow proxy models with features related to material balance and conservation laws.
  • E2CO uses data-driven reduction and DNNs for simultaneous state and input-output matching.
  • The non-intrusive method does not require access to reservoir simulator internal structure.
  • POD can be extended by augmenting state space to yield a bilinear system.
  • Autoencoders can linearize reservoir equations on a reduced manifold through a Koopman-operator view.

Important Terms

Porous Media Flow

Flow through materials with pores, such as oil, gas, or water movement in reservoirs.

Reservoir Digital Twin

A fast computational representation of a reservoir used for monitoring, optimization, and control.

E2CO

Embed to Control and Observe, a non-intrusive method combining reduction and ML for state and input-output matching.

Koopman Operator

A linear operator acting on observables of a nonlinear dynamical system.

Manifold Linearization

Learning a transformed low-dimensional representation where nonlinear dynamics become closer to linear.

Connections Across The Series

  • This lecture connects Koopman theory, autoencoder ROMs, and industrial digital twins in a reservoir setting.
  • It also complements learned simulator talks by focusing on non-intrusive deployment alongside existing simulators.
  • It reinforces the series theme that the useful latent space is one where control and prediction become easier.

Practical Caution

A learned linear manifold may work only over the operating envelope represented in the training data. Closed-loop reservoir control needs monitoring for when the latent linearization stops matching the simulator or field data.

Takeaway

The first-principles lesson is that a digital twin needs a representation where the control problem is cheap. Manifold linearization is a way to make nonlinear reservoir physics operational.
