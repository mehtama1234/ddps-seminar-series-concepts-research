Talk 87 · University of California, Los Angeles

Khalid Jawed: Reduced Order Modeling And Inverse Design Of Flexible Structures

This lecture uses neural networks and neural ODE ideas to build reduced models for highly deformable structures and to solve inverse design problems for shape-morphing systems.

YouTube source · Talk page

Core Problem

Highly deformable structures involve nonlinear geometry and nonlinear material behavior. Full fine-grained simulations can be expensive, while inverse design for target 3D shapes may require painful trial and error across complex local fabrication or actuation choices.

First-Principles Model

A flexible structure may have a lower-dimensional representation that captures its dominant deformation. For a slinky, the helix axis can serve as a reduced state instead of the full helical rod. A neural network trained on Discrete Elastic Rod simulations stores the effective elastic forces in that reduced representation. Neural ODE ideas then let the reduced dynamics be simulated efficiently. For morphing structures, ML maps design or actuation choices to target shapes for inverse design.

Key Claims

  • Highly deformable structures require simulation tools for nonlinear geometry and nonlinear material behavior.
  • Neural networks can reduce variables and accelerate simulation by orders of magnitude.
  • A slinky's deformation can often be captured by its helix axis as a reduced-order representation.
  • Neural networks can store elastic forces in the reduced representation.
  • Training data can come from fine-grained Discrete Elastic Rod simulations.
  • Shape-morphing structures motivate inverse design from planar patterns to target 3D shapes.

Important Terms

Highly Deformable Structure

A structure that undergoes large shape changes requiring nonlinear mechanics.

Reduced-Order Representation

A lower-dimensional description that captures the dominant structural state.

Neural ODE

A neural model representing continuous-time dynamics through differential equations.

Discrete Elastic Rods

A simulation framework for slender elastic structures such as rods, ropes, and helices.

Inverse Design

Finding design variables that produce a desired target behavior or shape.

Connections Across The Series

  • This lecture connects ROMs, neural ODEs, and inverse design in a tangible mechanics setting.
  • It also complements manufacturing and materials talks because geometry, fabrication, and mechanics interact.
  • It reinforces the idea that good reduced variables often come from physical insight, not only from data compression.

Practical Caution

A reduced structural coordinate is valid only when it captures the deformation modes used in deployment. If the structure bends, twists, contacts, or buckles outside the training regime, the learned reduced force law may fail.

Takeaway

The first-principles lesson is that inverse design of flexible structures starts with the right reduced mechanical coordinates. ML accelerates the force law once the representation is physically meaningful.
