Talk 55 · Georgia Institute of Technology

Molei Tao: Trustworthy Learning Of Mechanical Systems And Stiefel Optimization

This lecture shows how hard-wiring geometric structure such as symplecticity into learned mechanical dynamics improves long-time prediction, and connects dynamics ideas to optimization on the Stiefel manifold.

YouTube source · Talk page

Core Problem

Mechanical systems preserve geometric structures such as energy behavior, momentum, and symplecticity. Standard neural predictors can fit short trajectories while violating these structures, causing long-time drift. Optimization on constrained manifolds such as the Stiefel manifold has a related problem: algorithms should respect the geometry of the space they move on.

First-Principles Model

A mechanical system is not just a sequence of states; it is a flow with preserved geometric structure. Symplectic learning builds this preservation into the model so the learned time evolution remains faithful over long horizons. For Stiefel optimization, the variable lies on the manifold of orthonormal frames, so gradient dynamics and momentum acceleration must be designed on the manifold rather than in unconstrained Euclidean space.

Key Claims

  • Physics hard-wired into deep learning models can improve trustworthy prediction of mechanical dynamics.
  • Mechanical systems preserve structures such as energy, momentum, and symplecticity.
  • Symplecticity is an underlying geometric property of Hamiltonian dynamics.
  • Hard-wiring symplectic structure can provably improve long-time prediction accuracy.
  • The interaction between dynamics and machine learning also informs optimization algorithms.
  • Momentum-accelerated gradient descent can be constructed on Riemannian manifolds, with the Stiefel manifold as a key example.

Important Terms

Symplecticity

A geometric structure preserved by Hamiltonian mechanical dynamics.

Hamiltonian Dynamics

A formulation of mechanics based on position, momentum, and energy-like Hamiltonian functions.

Long-Time Prediction

Forecasting a dynamical system over many time steps where small structural errors can accumulate.

Stiefel Manifold

The set of matrices with orthonormal columns, common in constrained optimization problems.

Riemannian Optimization

Optimization methods designed for variables constrained to curved manifolds.

Connections Across The Series

  • This lecture connects to the structure-preserving thread across PINNs, neural ODEs, and scientific ML.
  • It complements uncertainty and trustworthiness talks by showing one way to make extrapolation safer: preserve geometry.
  • It also links dynamics and optimization, a recurring hidden theme in neural PDE solvers and manifold learning.

Practical Caution

Hard-wiring structure helps only when the assumed structure matches the system. If forcing symplecticity on dissipative or driven systems, the model can preserve the wrong geometry.

Takeaway

The first-principles lesson is that trustworthy dynamics learning starts by respecting the geometry of the system. Prediction accuracy over time follows from preserving the right invariants.
