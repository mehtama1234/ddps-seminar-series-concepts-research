Talk 36 · Columbia University

Steve Sun: Data-Driven Constitutive Updates From Model-Free Poroelasticity To Level Set Plasticity

This lecture examines data-driven constitutive modeling, from model-free poroelastic updates to interpretable neural level-set plasticity.

YouTube source · Talk page

Core Problem

Continuum mechanics simulations need constitutive laws relating strains, stresses, pressures, velocities, and internal variables. In complex materials, these laws may be unknown, expensive to calibrate, or only partially supported by experimental data. A purely model-free update can use data directly, but limited data demands interpretable and physics-compatible structure.

First-Principles Model

A constitutive update supplies the local material response inside a global mechanics solve. In poroelasticity, data may define the relationship between strain and effective stress, and between pressure gradient and Darcy velocity. A model-free method searches the data manifold for admissible local states, while neural or level-set models approximate yield surfaces or plastic updates when less data are available.

Key Claims

  • Data-driven constitutive modeling has roots going back to early neural-network constitutive laws.
  • The goal is to make constitutive laws interpretable and workable with limited data.
  • Poroelastic simulations need both solid and hydraulic constitutive relationships.
  • Model-free mechanics can switch between data-driven and model-based updates depending on available data.
  • Distance-minimization and data-structure acceleration such as KD-tree search can support data-driven updates.
  • Level-set plasticity can learn interpretable macroscopic surrogate plasticity models from subscale simulations.

Important Terms

Constitutive Law

A material model relating local mechanical variables such as stress, strain, pressure, and flow.

Model-Free Mechanics

A simulation approach that uses measured material data directly instead of a prescribed constitutive equation.

Poroelasticity

Coupled deformation and fluid-flow behavior in porous materials.

Constitutive Manifold

The set of physically admissible local material states represented by data or a learned model.

Level-Set Plasticity

A plasticity representation where a learned or defined level set describes yielding or material state transitions.

Connections Across The Series

  • This talk adds material modeling to the DDPS stack: the uncertain learned object is often the constitutive law, not the whole PDE.
  • It connects to hybrid twins because material updates are a natural place to combine data and mechanistic balance laws.
  • It also connects to model-constrained learning because the global PDE solve constrains what local learned material responses are admissible.

Practical Caution

A learned constitutive model can destabilize a global mechanics solve if it violates admissibility, consistency, or energy behavior. The local material model must be validated inside the coupled simulation, not only on isolated stress-strain data.

Takeaway

The first-principles lesson is that data-driven mechanics often lives at the constitutive update. Learn the material response, but keep it compatible with the global physics.
