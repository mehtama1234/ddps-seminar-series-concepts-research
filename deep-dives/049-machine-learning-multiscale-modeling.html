Talk 49 · Peking University / Princeton University

Weinan E: Machine Learning And Multi-Scale Modeling

This lecture explains how machine learning helps multiscale modeling by representing complex functions and closures that classical hand-built models struggle to express.

YouTube source · Talk page

Core Problem

Multiscale modeling aims to connect quantum, atomistic, coarse-grained, continuum, and turbulence-level descriptions into practical simulations of properties of interest. The idea is compelling, but hard problems remain because effective models and closure relations can be too complex to derive or parameterize manually.

First-Principles Model

Each scale has a model that is useful in its own regime. The multiscale task is to pass information between scales: fine models supply effective laws, closures, potentials, or constitutive relations for coarser models. Machine learning expands the representational toolkit for these interscale maps, allowing learned potentials, coarse-grained dynamics, non-Newtonian constitutive models, and reduced PDE models.

Key Claims

  • Multiscale modeling is an attempt to unify physical models across scales for accurate simulation protocols.
  • Its practical success has been limited by difficulty representing complex functions and complex models.
  • Machine learning offers flexible representations for closures and effective models.
  • Applications include molecular dynamics, coarse-grained molecular dynamics, non-Newtonian fluid dynamics, and PDE model reduction.
  • The hierarchy of physical models spans quantum mechanics, density functional theory, molecular dynamics, coarse-grained models, continuum mechanics, and turbulence models.
  • The central promise is not replacing physics, but learning the pieces that connect physical descriptions across scales.

Important Terms

Multiscale Modeling

A framework for linking models defined at different physical scales to predict macroscopic quantities of interest.

Closure Model

An approximate relation that represents unresolved fine-scale effects inside a coarser model.

Coarse-Grained Molecular Dynamics

A molecular simulation approach that groups fine degrees of freedom into larger effective units.

Non-Newtonian Fluid

A fluid whose stress-strain relationship cannot be described by a constant viscosity Newtonian law.

Effective Model

A model at one scale that summarizes relevant effects of more detailed lower-scale physics.

Connections Across The Series

  • It also connects to materials ML because learned potentials are one concrete multiscale bridge.

Practical Caution

A learned closure can be accurate inside its training regime and still break conservation, stability, or transfer across scale if it is not embedded consistently in the larger simulator.

Takeaway

The first-principles lesson is that multiscale ML is about learning interfaces between physical descriptions. The model is valuable when it lets a trusted simulation reason across scales.
