Talk 114 · UC Berkeley

Aditi Krishnapriyan: Bridging Numerical Methods and Deep Learning with Physics-Constrained Differentiable Solvers

This lecture explains how differentiable solvers can connect neural PDE learning with classical numerical methods and physics constraints.

YouTube source · Talk page

Core Problem

Spatiotemporal ML models must make valid predictions under limited data, but purely neural solvers can ignore numerical structure while purely classical solvers may not learn from data. The challenge is to train models that exploit both learned representations and physics-constrained computation.

First-Principles Model

A differentiable solver makes a numerical method part of the trainable computation graph. This allows gradients to flow through a PDE solve, spectral method, or constrained optimization layer. The talk discusses self-supervised learning for changing the learning basis with spectral methods, as well as simulation-in-the-loop approaches where PDE-constrained optimization becomes a neural-network layer.

Key Claims

  • Machine learning is increasingly important for spatiotemporal modeling.
  • Limited-data settings require learning strategies that preserve prediction validity.
  • Self-supervised learning can change the basis of learning for spectral neural PDE solvers.
  • Simulation-in-the-loop methods can incorporate PDE-constrained optimization as a layer.
  • Differentiable solvers allow numerical methods and neural networks to be trained together.
  • The goal is broad learning strategies that apply across systems and architectures.

Important Terms

Differentiable Solver

A numerical solver whose operations are differentiable so it can be embedded in gradient-based learning.

Physics-Constrained Learning

Learning where physical equations or constraints restrict the model's predictions or training process.

PDE-Constrained Optimization

Optimization in which feasible solutions must satisfy a PDE.

Simulation-in-the-Loop

A learning setup where simulation is included directly inside training or inference.

Spectral Method

A numerical method representing functions in frequency or basis-function space.

Connections Across The Series

  • This talk connects neural PDE solvers, PINNs, differentiable programming, and numerical analysis.
  • It complements physics-informed learning by embedding solvers rather than only adding residual losses.
  • It also links to inverse design and control because gradients through solvers enable optimization.

Practical Caution

A differentiable solver still inherits the stability, discretization, and modeling assumptions of the underlying numerical method. Differentiability alone does not make the physics correct.

Takeaway

The first-principles lesson is that solver structure can become part of the neural architecture, allowing learning to respect physics while still adapting from data.
