Talk 95 · BNZ Energy Inc.

Michael Glinsky: A Physics-Based ROM Capturing The Topology Of Dynamical Manifolds

This lecture presents a physics-based reduced-order modeling approach inspired by scattering transforms, renormalization ideas, and topology-preserving projections of dynamical manifolds.

YouTube source · Talk page

Core Problem

High-energy-density physics simulations such as pulsed power liner implosions are expensive and nonlinear, yet practical work needs fast surrogates. A ROM that only interpolates state vectors may miss the manifold topology and scale-dependent structure of the dynamics.

First-Principles Model

The proposed Heisenberg Scattering Transformation applies a structured deep convolutional transform inspired by Mallat's scattering transform and a Hamiltonian generating functional. It projects dynamics into a renormalization or S-matrix basis where the evolution is constrained to a low-dimensional complex linear subspace. PCA identifies this subspace, and an encoder-decoder MLP quantifies topology through an analytic function on it.

Key Claims

  • The approach is inspired by Mallat's scattering transformation and a canonical Heisenberg-style perspective on dynamics.
  • A logarithmic generating functional of the Hamiltonian operates on the system state or statistical distribution.
  • The transform projects dynamics onto a renormalization or scattering-matrix basis.
  • The low-dimensional complex linear subspace can be identified with PCA.
  • Topology is quantified using an MLP with decoder/encoder structure approximating an analytic function on the subspace.
  • The method is applied as a fast surrogate for 2D pulsed power liner implosions, including MagLIF.

Important Terms

Scattering Transformation

A structured wavelet/convolutional transform that builds stable hierarchical representations.

Heisenberg Scattering Transformation

The talk's proposed physics-based transform for representing dynamics in a renormalization/scattering basis.

Renormalization Basis

A basis associated with how physics changes across scale.

Dynamical Manifold Topology

The qualitative geometric structure of the low-dimensional manifold on which dynamics evolve.

MagLIF

Magnetized Liner Inertial Fusion, a pulsed-power fusion concept used as an application setting.

Connections Across The Series

  • This lecture connects manifold learning, ROMs, topology, and physics-based transforms.
  • It complements charting dynamics and information geometry by emphasizing that the reduced space has topology, not just coordinates.
  • It also links to high-energy-density surrogate modeling, where preserving structure matters for fast prediction.

Practical Caution

Topology-aware transforms are powerful but specialized. The assumed transform structure must match the physics; otherwise the ROM can preserve the wrong geometric features.

Takeaway

The first-principles lesson is that some ROMs must preserve dynamical topology and scale structure, not just minimize projection error.
