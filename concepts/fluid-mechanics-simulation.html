Fluid Mechanics Simulation

Fluid mechanics simulation models flows, shocks, turbulence, and conservation laws using numerical methods, learned surrogates, or hybrid methods.

Ordinary Problem

Fluids create multiscale, nonlinear, instability-prone dynamics that are costly to simulate and difficult to learn robustly.

Naive Picture

The naive view is that a neural network can simply replace a simulator once enough data is available.

Why It Fails

Physical simulation problems mix equations, data, discretization, stability, uncertainty, and scientific validity. A generic ML benchmark can hide failures that matter in the real system.

First-Principles Move

A flow model must respect transport, conservation, stability, and scale interactions; otherwise small errors can propagate into wrong dynamics.

What Breaks Without It

Without this concept, it is easy to build a model that looks accurate on examples but fails under new parameters, longer time horizons, or scientific constraints.

Summit Role

This theme organizes DDPS seminars related to fluid mechanics simulation and gives us a first-pass route for later deep dives.
