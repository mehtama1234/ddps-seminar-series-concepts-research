Uncertainty And Robustness

Uncertainty and robustness address whether learned simulation models remain reliable under sparse data, out-of-distribution parameters, numerical error, and long rollouts.

Ordinary Problem

A surrogate can be accurate on average yet fail exactly where scientific or engineering decisions are most sensitive.

Naive Picture

The naive view is that a neural network can simply replace a simulator once enough data is available.

Why It Fails

Physical simulation problems mix equations, data, discretization, stability, uncertainty, and scientific validity. A generic ML benchmark can hide failures that matter in the real system.

First-Principles Move

A model should communicate what it knows, remain stable under perturbations, and be tested where errors would change the conclusion.

What Breaks Without It

Without this concept, it is easy to build a model that looks accurate on examples but fails under new parameters, longer time horizons, or scientific constraints.

Summit Role

This theme organizes DDPS seminars related to uncertainty and robustness and gives us a first-pass route for later deep dives.
