First-Principles Thesis

Data-driven simulation is not just replacing solvers with neural nets. It is a scientific modeling problem: governing equations, numerical stability, training data, discretization, uncertainty, and downstream decisions must fit together.

The seminar series is useful because it shows the field from multiple angles: why PINNs fail, how reduced models work, how differentiable simulators enable inverse design, and how learned surrogates interact with physics.

Concept Route

Physics-Informed Learning

Physics-informed learning trains models while constraining them with known governing equations, conservation laws, boundary conditions, or residual penalties.

Reduced-Order Modeling

Reduced-order modeling builds lower-dimensional surrogates of expensive physical simulations while preserving the dominant dynamics needed for prediction or control.

Differentiable Simulation

Differentiable simulation exposes gradients through a simulator so parameters, controls, shapes, or models can be optimized using gradient-based methods.

Operator Learning

Operator learning trains models to map between functions, such as from initial conditions or coefficients to full solution fields.

Fluid Mechanics Simulation

Fluid mechanics simulation models flows, shocks, turbulence, and conservation laws using numerical methods, learned surrogates, or hybrid methods.

Hybrid Twins

Hybrid twins combine mechanistic simulation with learned components and live data to represent, predict, and update physical systems.

Scientific Machine Learning

Scientific machine learning uses ML to accelerate, infer, control, or augment models of physical, biological, and engineering systems.

Uncertainty And Robustness

Uncertainty and robustness address whether learned simulation models remain reliable under sparse data, out-of-distribution parameters, numerical error, and long rollouts.

Inverse Problems And Control

Inverse problems and control use observations or objectives to infer hidden parameters, states, forces, designs, or interventions.
