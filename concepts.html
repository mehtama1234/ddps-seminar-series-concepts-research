Physics-Informed Learning

Pure black-box models can fit data while violating physics, especially when measurements are sparse or extrapolation matters.

Reduced-Order Modeling

High-fidelity simulations can be too slow for design loops, optimization, uncertainty quantification, or real-time control.

data-driven physical simulation

Differentiable Simulation

Many scientific and engineering tasks ask not only what happens, but what input would produce a desired outcome.

data-driven physical simulation

Operator Learning

A simulator often needs to solve a family of related PDE problems, not just predict one fixed vector output.

data-driven physical simulation

Fluid Mechanics Simulation

Fluids create multiscale, nonlinear, instability-prone dynamics that are costly to simulate and difficult to learn robustly.

data-driven physical simulation

Hybrid Twins

Pure simulation may miss unknown effects, while pure data models may fail outside observed regimes.

data-driven physical simulation

Scientific Machine Learning

Scientific problems often have limited data, strong priors, expensive simulators, and high penalties for physically invalid predictions.

data-driven physical simulation

Uncertainty And Robustness

A surrogate can be accurate on average yet fail exactly where scientific or engineering decisions are most sensitive.

data-driven physical simulation

Inverse Problems And Control

In practice we often observe effects and need to recover causes, or choose actions that make a physical system behave a desired way.
